---
title: 'What a note is for'
publishDate: 2026-09-21T00:00:00Z
kind: permanent
stage: seedling
---

A note is where I give an idea enough room to become useful. It is longer than a log, but it does not need to be a finished argument. I use notes to make a distinction, capture a lesson, respond to something I have read, or preserve a question that deserves another visit.

The stage matters because ideas have different jobs. A seedling is an invitation to keep looking. A budding note has a shape and a few connections. An evergreen note has earned its place through revision and reuse. None of those labels is a judgment of quality. They simply tell me how much attention an idea has received.

A good note should be understandable when I return to it later. It should say what I think, what changed my mind, and where the next question might lead.
