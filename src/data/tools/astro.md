Astro is a good fit for this site because writing and reading come first. It keeps the default output lean while still leaving room for interactive pieces when they are genuinely useful.

I particularly appreciate the way Astro treats content collections, Markdown, and static generation as first-class parts of the workflow. That makes it easier to keep the site understandable as it grows.
