Today I started turning the AstroWind foundation into something that belongs to me. The first useful decision was not a color or a component. It was deciding what the site is for: a place to develop ideas about leadership, technology, operations, and change.

Astro is a good fit because the site can stay close to the content. I can begin with a Markdown file, connect it to a [note about the site structure](../notes/astro-site-as-a-system.md), and later develop the pattern into an [article](../articles/building-an-astro-site-as-a-small-system.md). The [Astro site map](../maps/astro-site-building.md) collects the path.

The work feels more manageable when each decision leaves a small, inspectable trace.
