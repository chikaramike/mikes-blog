I am making mikes.blog as a place to put ideas before they are polished. Some thoughts will be useful only for a day. Others will grow into notes, articles, or maps that help me see a subject more clearly.

This is also a record of how I work: curious, practical, systems-minded, and interested in the distance between a good strategy and a useful result. I want this garden to hold that movement in public, one small piece at a time.
